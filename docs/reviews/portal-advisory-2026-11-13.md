## Ticket policy and notice scope

The portal says valid Hoboken monthly and weekly passes and FLEXPASS via Newark Broad St. for Midtown Direct customers are accepted to/from Penn Station NY November 15–30. One-way travel requires Penn Station NY tickets in that period; return journeys need the appropriate Penn Station NY tickets too. The notice does not extend the cutover's PATH, ferry or bus cross-honoring beyond its stated period.

The notice selects November 13, November 14 and post-November 14 guidance using Eastern midnight boundaries. After November 14 it reports the end of the published schedule period and the announced schedules, with the safety-testing condition. It never infers actual restoration from the calendar. November ticket guidance expires at December 1 Eastern midnight. Static HTML uses explicit dates and the same condition so readers without JavaScript do not see a stale October start announcement.

Only the portal policy and November 15 advisory registry records are refreshed. Other source dates, the homepage's October 11 full-review date, line-level data and historical blog articles retain their existing scope. Check official schedules and live alerts before travel.
