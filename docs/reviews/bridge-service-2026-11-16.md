# Portal North Bridge service review: Nov. 16, 2026

NJ Transit confirms that Portal North Bridge is in service. Its Nov. 16 press release says both tracks are operating after the final cutover. Its current rail alerts and restoration advisory list regular weekday schedules from Nov. 16 for all five lines covered here.

Sources checked:

- [Portal North Bridge project page](https://www.njtransit.com/portal).
- [NJ Transit and Amtrak full-service announcement](https://www.njtransit.com/press-releases/nj-transit-and-amtrak-mark-historic-milestone-portal-north-bridge-enters-full), Nov. 16.
- [Regular weekday restoration advisory](https://www.njtransit.com/service-advisory/2199523), first listed Nov. 15 and updated Nov. 16.
- Weekday timetables linked by that advisory, effective Nov. 15: [Montclair-Boonton](https://content.njtransit.com/sites/default/files/MC-WKDY-111526.pdf), [Morris & Essex](https://content.njtransit.com/sites/default/files/ME-WKDY-111526.pdf), [Northeast Corridor](https://content.njtransit.com/sites/default/files/NEC-WKDY-111526.pdf), [North Jersey Coast](https://content.njtransit.com/sites/default/files/NJCL-WKDY-111526.pdf), and [Raritan Valley](https://content.njtransit.com/sites/default/files/RVL-WKDY-111526.pdf).
- [Ticket rules](https://www.njtransit.com/portalcutover) for the Nov. 15–30 acceptance of eligible Hoboken monthly and weekly passes and FLEXPASS via Newark Broad Street.
- [Current travel alerts](https://www.njtransit.com/travel-alerts-to).

The timetables support weekday Midtown Direct service to Penn Station NY, retained Hoboken trains, Coast Line connections, and selected Raritan Valley one-seat trips. RVL's NY symbol means no transfer; C means change at Newark Penn. These statements describe published service. Whether every scheduled departure operated on Nov. 16 is unconfirmed. Train totals, every stop, and every live trip were not audited. Other maintenance and service alerts still apply.
