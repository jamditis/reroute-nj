Portal North Bridge is in service

By Joe Amditis Nov. 16, 2026

Both tracks of Portal North Bridge are in service. NJ Transit resumed regular weekday schedules Nov. 16, 2026. The cutover phases are complete.

Current service by line

  • Montclair-Boonton: Weekday Midtown Direct service to Penn Station NY is back in the regular schedule. Hoboken trains remain; check your station and train.
  • Morris & Essex (Morristown / Gladstone): Morristown Line and Gladstone Branch weekday Midtown Direct service to Penn Station NY is back in the regular schedule. Hoboken trains remain.
  • Northeast Corridor: The regular weekday timetable replaces the cutover reductions. Trains serve Penn Station NY; check the current schedule and alerts.
  • North Jersey Coast: The regular weekday timetable replaces the cutover reductions. Check whether your train runs through to Penn Station NY or requires a Long Branch or Newark transfer.
  • Raritan Valley: The regular weekday timetable includes selected one-seat rides to Penn Station NY. Other trips require a transfer at Newark Penn; check your train.

These are published schedules, not confirmation that every departure operated. Other work can still cause delays. Check NJ Transit alerts before each trip.
